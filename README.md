# Swag Shopping Mall

Swag Shopping Mall is a responsive online store designed to bring everyday products from wholesale sourcing to customers at approachable prices. The storefront pairs a friendly, colorful shopping experience with a curated range of useful finds for home and daily life.

## The store

The catalog brings together groceries, kitchen essentials, home and living products, fashion, and footwear. Product listings present key details such as imagery, descriptions, availability, and pricing. Fashion and footwear items can offer selectable sizes, with prices shown for each available option.

The storefront is designed for comfortable browsing on desktop and mobile, with a warm visual identity and clear product presentation from discovery through purchase.
